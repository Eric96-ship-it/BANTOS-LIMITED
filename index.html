Clear Data

Each action is independent and requires confirmation.

Remove specific data categories without affecting the others. Nothing is deleted until you confirm.

Clear Trip Records Remove all saved trip records. Linked expense records are kept but shown as unlinked.
Clear Expenses Remove all saved expense records. Trip records are kept exactly as they are.
Clear Invoices Remove the saved invoice history only. Trips, expenses, routes and settings are untouched.
Clear All Data Wipe every record — trips, expenses, invoice history, routes and settings. This cannot be undone.
